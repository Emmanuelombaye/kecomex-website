About KecomeX

Who We Are

KecomeX Tours and Safaris Limited is a premier travel company based in Diani Beach, Kenya and a proud subsidiary of KecomeX Business Club International. We craft unforgettable safaris, ocean adventures, city tours and cultural experiences across Kenya.

Our coastal headquarters gives clients direct access to pristine beaches, marine life and world heritage sites—delivered with world-class service and safety.

Mission

To provide authentic, safe and memorable travel experiences while supporting local communities, environmental conservation and sustainable tourism.

Vision

To be East Africa’s most trusted and innovative travel and safari partner for international and domestic tourists.
